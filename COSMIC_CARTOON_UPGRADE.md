## 🌟 What's New: Cosmic Effects

### 1. **Motion Lines Effect** (.motion-lines)
- Speed lines appear on hover
- Right side of element shows horizontal dashes
- Yellow (#FFD600) animation
- Perfect for primary buttons
- Creates sense of movement and energy

### 2. **Zoom Effect** (.zoom-effect)
- Yellow border pulse on hover
- Expands outward like comic book zoom
- 0.6s animation
- Used on cards and interactive elements

### 3. **Peace Vibe** (.peace-vibe)
- ✌️ emoji appears on hover
- Tribute to the peace-hand logo
- Subtle scale + rotate animation
- Top-right corner placement

### 4. **Squiggle Line** (.squiggle)
- Animated underline effect
- Yellow dashed pattern
- Scales from left to right on hover
- Great for headings and CTAs

### 5. **Comic Burst** (.comic-burst)
- Radial gradient background burst
- Yellow spotlights (20% & 80% positions)
- Subtle 15% opacity
- Creates depth and focus

### 6. **Sticker Effect** (.sticker)
- Slight rotation (-2deg default)
- Rotates opposite way on hover (+2deg)
- Scales up to 1.05
- Shadow increases
- Like physical stickers moving

### 7. **Star Burst** (.star-burst)
- ✨ emoji appears at top-right
- Twinkle animation (scale + rotate)
- 2s infinite loop
- Adds sparkle to titles

### 8. **Outline Text** (.outline-text-filled)
- Yellow fill with black stroke
- 3px stroke width
- Font-weight: 900
- Uppercase transform
- Perfect for hero titles

### 9. **Thought Bubble** (.thought-bubble)
- Rounded speech bubble
- Small circles trail (comic style)
- White bg with black border
- Can be used for tooltips/callouts

### 10. **Exclaim Effect** (.exclaim)
- Giant "!" appears top-right
- Yellow with black shadow
- Bounce animation
- Emphasizes important elements

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

### Core Principles
1. **Bold & Playful**: Like Duolingo, Slack, modern SaaS
2. **High Energy**: Motion, rotation, bouncing
3. **Peace Hand Spirit**: Fun, friendly, approachable ✌️
4. **Comic Book Aesthetic**: Hard shadows, thick borders, flat colors
5. **Instant Feedback**: Every interaction feels alive

### Visual Hierarchy
- **Yellow** = Primary actions, important elements
- **Black** = Borders, text, structure
- **White** = Clean backgrounds, breathing room
- **Emojis** = Personality and quick scanning
